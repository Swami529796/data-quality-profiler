How the score is worked out

Each column gets four part-scores from 0 to 100:

  • Completeness ({{ (weights.completeness * 100)|int }}%): share of rows that have a value. Placeholders such as N/A count as missing.
  • Validity ({{ (weights.validity * 100)|int }}%): share of values that fit the column type, follow the usual pattern and pass the rules.
  • Uniqueness ({{ (weights.uniqueness * 100)|int }}%): share of values that do not repeat an earlier one. Only for the key, identifier-like columns and columns with a unique rule.
  • Consistency ({{ (weights.consistency * 100)|int }}%): share of values free of stray spaces, mixed capital letters, and numbers or dates written differently from the rest.

The column score is the weighted average of the parts that apply. The overall score is the plain average of the column scores, reduced by the share of exact duplicate rows. Outliers and constant columns are listed but do not change the score. A high score means none of these checks found much; it does not prove the data is correct.
